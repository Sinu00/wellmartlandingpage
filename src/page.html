The rules, in plain words.

Please read these before you join. Payments are non-refundable, and only members who complete all four months are eligible for prizes.

01Scheme duration
The duration of this scheme is 4 months.
02Monthly payment
Each member must pay ₹500 per month continuously for 4 months, making the total payment ₹2,000.
03Special surprise gift
Members who pay the full 4-month amount of ₹2,000 in a single payment will receive a special surprise gift.

The draw is conducted every month at the WellMart office in the presence of the participants and the public. It is also broadcast through YouTube Live, and winner announcements and updates are shared through WhatsApp and Instagram.
